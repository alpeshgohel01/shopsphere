# Image generation prompts

Use these prompts to produce royalty-free assets under `public/images/auth/`. Export WebP or AVIF, compressed, each under 250 KB. Photorealistic, natural light, **no text, no logos, no distorted hands or faces**.

Swap images with a one-line path change in `src/components/auth/AuthLayout.jsx` (hero) or the relevant page component.

## 1. Auth hero (portrait 4:5) — `hero.webp`

> Photorealistic portrait 4:5 of a modern bright co-working space, a professional working on a laptop, shallow depth of field, warm natural window light, 35mm lens look, no text, no logos, no distorted hands or faces.

## 2. Auth hero alternative (4:5) — `hero-alt.webp`

> Photorealistic overhead shot 4:5 of a clean desk with a laptop, coffee, and a plant, soft morning light, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos.

## 3. Dashboard empty state (16:9) — `dashboard-empty.webp`

> Photorealistic minimal abstract soft-gradient workspace scene, 16:9, calm neutrals with subtle teal accent light, no people, no text, no logos.

## 4. Avatar fallbacks (1:1) — `avatar-01.webp` … `avatar-06.webp`

> Set of 6 diverse, natural headshots, square 1:1, neutral soft background, natural window light, photorealistic, no text, no logos, no distorted hands or faces. Each person distinct in age, gender presentation, and ethnicity.
